Our Philosophy

Water remembers what the body forgets. Every ritual at Velours begins the same way — slow immersion, low light, the sound of nothing at all — because rest is not an amenity. It is a discipline we have spent a decade refining.

We keep our rituals few and our attention undivided. No phones past the entry, no clocks past the lobby, no music but the pools themselves. What's left is simply you, and an hour that belongs to no one else.
